About the project

A functional prototype for a monitored, single-camera space

SENTRY is built for a controlled setting — a classroom, lab, or office desk — where one camera needs to handle attendance, recognize unfamiliar faces, watch for unusual movement, and read the room's mood, without depending on heavy ML infrastructure. Detection results are turned into short, human-readable alerts, and every event is logged for later review on the dashboard.
